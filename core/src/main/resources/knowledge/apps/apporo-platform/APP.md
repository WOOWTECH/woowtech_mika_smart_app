# Apporo platform

Apporo platform is the Apporo business application based on Odoo. It is separate from the connected-space app and computer-agent client. Inspect installed apps and ask the user to select their configured server and account. Login, passwords and verification are handled by the user.

Available modules depend on the server and account. Do not assume Maintenance, Project, Inventory or a particular dashboard is installed. Read actual navigation labels and visible records. Use the selected company and project as context; ask when there are multiple matches.

For a task summary, inspect assigned items and dates without editing records. Distinguish missing access from an empty task list. Report visible status and sources; do not infer completion from a notification or draft.

Creating or editing records is a separate action from reading them. Confirm ambiguous recipients, companies, assets and amounts. Stop at payment or order approval boundaries, even if the workflow is called procurement or maintenance. Do not submit invoices, purchase orders or bulk changes merely to prepare a summary.

This guide describes the product relationship and safe discovery approach. Specific screens and modules still require runtime verification.
