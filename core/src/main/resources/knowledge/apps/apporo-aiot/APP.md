# Apporo aiot

Apporo aiot connects to a configured Home Assistant-based space. It is not Apporo smart or Apporo platform. First inspect installed apps. Ask the user for their server or let them choose a discovered server; never guess a public address. The user handles sign-in and verification.

Dashboards, room names, cards and permissions differ by installation. Read the current view rather than relying on fixed coordinates. Identify the site, room and device together. A card tap may toggle a device immediately. Prefer opening details when inspecting status, and report timestamps and unavailable readings explicitly.

Before changing lighting or climate, establish the requested device and desired state. Do not operate locks, alarms, access gates or safety equipment without a clear user request and the applicable confirmation. After a change, re-read the state; a successful tap does not prove the physical device changed. Report an offline device as unavailable, not broken.

Energy and historical data exist only when configured. Summaries require actual readings and units. Do not promise native predictive maintenance or building analytics from the assistant alone. These are product-level capabilities that depend on the installed system.

This guide is based on the sibling app architecture, not a device-verified screen walkthrough. Stop and inspect when the actual screen differs.
