# Apporo assets

Launcher and notification vector geometry comes from the existing Apporo smart brand definition (`woowtech/brands/apporo.json`, logo.paths) and its official `apporo-mark-source.svg`. No new logo was invented.

The paths are scaled into the adaptive icon safe area. Gray tile #4D4D4D and white mark distinguish the launcher; UI accents use #8B6B24 on light surfaces and #C49E53 on dark surfaces. Rendering and contrast still need device review.

Apporo trademark ownership is separate from the Mika source license. Original engine and third-party attributions remain in the app.
